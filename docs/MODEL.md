# MODEL — what the physics actually is

Every screen that shows simulation output is generated by the modules described here. All coefficients live in `data/calibration/defaults.js` and are exposed in Settings → Calibration. **None of this is CFD** and none of it is fitted to real team data: it is a reduced-order engineering game model tuned for plausible, teachable behaviour.

## Chassis shape — the sculpted body (src/core/carShape.js + geometry V4.2)

Since 4.2 the BODY IS A SHAPE, not a set of dials. `carShape.js` stores two curves
over 12 stations from nose (t=0) to tail (t=1):

- `heights[i]` — silhouette height above the floor, 60–700 mm
- `widths[i]`  — planform half-width incl. sidepod mass, 40–660 mm

`sampleCurve` interpolates each curve with Catmull-Rom splines; `geometry.js`
lofts the mesh directly from the SAME sampled curves, so what you drag in the
Shape Studio is exactly what gets meshed and measured (WYSIWYG by construction).
`sanitizeShape` clamps rogue input; `smoothShape` runs 3-point smoothing passes
that keep the endpoints.

`shapeAero(shape, model)` integrates 48 superellipse sections of the curves into
physical descriptors consumed by the solver — **no arbitrary multipliers**:

| descriptor | meaning | solver use |
|---|---|---|
| `frontalAreaM2` | true body frontal area (superellipse sections × skin factor) | body drag scales with it (`1.02·A·f(fineness)`) |
| `fineness` | length ÷ equivalent diameter | fineness drag factor, clamped 0.72–1.3 |
| `deckSlope` | rear-deck upwash angle | body-lift gain (2.1 per unit slope) |
| `noseSlope` | nose droop beyond 7 % | small body-lift gain (0.35 per unit) |
| `maxWidthMm` | widest half-width ×2 | width factor vs 1260 mm reference |

The old body-metric parameters (`bodyWidth`, `noseTaper`, `sidepodShoulder`,
`rearContraction`) no longer influence the solver — the sculpted shape is the
single body input. The DESIGN screen hides them; the Shape Studio owns the body.
Sidepods are part of the sculpted planform (one form, like the 2026 cars), so the
`sidepod` mesh group no longer exists; rims got their own `rims` group for silver
material. Share codes: `APEX2-…` packs both curves quantised to 8 bits/station
(48 bytes) plus the APEX1 params body; `APEX1-…` codes still decode (no shape).

## Aerodynamics (src/core/aero.js)

`F = ½ · ρ · V² · C·A` throughout. `q = ½ρV²`.

### Front wing
- Effective AoA: `α = fwAngle + 0.35·fwFlap` (flap partially effective), camber and element-count modifiers, span×chord area scaling.
- Ground proximity gain: `1 + G·exp(−h/h₀)` (stronger load near the ground).
- **Soft stall**: logistic `1/(1+exp(−(α−α_stall)/s))` — load plateaus and decays past the stall incidence instead of growing forever.
- Drag = induced `CL²/(π·AR·e)` + profile term.
- **Flex feedback (§21)**: load → twist (`compliance · kN`) → reduced effective AoA → new load, iterated 4×.

### Floor + diffuser (ground effect, §17)
- Operating point `x = h_avg / h_opt` where `h_opt` scales with `√(throat area)` and tunnel depth.
- Response `x·e^(1−x)`: rises to the tunnel optimum, decays beyond it — the classic venturi hump.
- **Choke**: below ~10 mm an extra `1−exp(−(h−h_min)/h₀)` collapse — very low ride height is a cliff, never infinite load.
- **Diffuser separation**: smoothstep past 13°, −55% recovery by 19° (legal to 17° — legal ≠ good).
- **Rake**: gain `1 + 0.25·rake − 0.09·rake²` — sweet spot ≈ 1.4°.
- **Yaw**: `loss = max·(1−exp(−(ψ/ψ_crit)²))` — underbody is the yaw-fragile component; balance migrates forward.

### Rear wing
- Same thin-airfoil skeleton + **two explicit interaction terms (§16, §53)**: FW wake loss (scales with front-wing load) and diffuser upwash gain (scales with diffuser load). DRS multiplies effective AoA and cuts induced drag.
- Dirty air: separate FW/floor/RW loss factors + small drag reduction (following mode, §62).

### Body / wheels / cooling
- Body CL·A from width, taper and shoulders; drag ledger = FW, floor/edge, RW, bodywork, wheels, cooling (inlet^1.3 with outlet-flow factor).
- Thermal: capacity vs demand at speed; margin flag below thresholds. Smaller inlets are **never** automatically better (§23).

### Balance & ledger
- `balance%F = (FW + floorFrontShare·floor + bodyFrontShare·body) / total`. The contribution ledger lists each component's isolated force plus the two interaction deltas; they sum to the reported totals (tested).

## Stability (src/core/stability.js)

- **Porpoising (§20)**: 1-DOF heave oscillator, `m·z̈ = −k·z − c·ż − K_a·z(t−τ)` where `K_a = ∂DF/∂h` from the floor curve and τ ≈ 45 ms flow lag (damper-rate dependent). Envelope growth between successive peaks = risk %. Restoring-slope-with-delay = negative damping — the real mechanism, not a dice roll.
- Sweeps: ride height (peak + stall edge), yaw (loss + balance migration + lateral force), speed (V² scaling + flex twist).

## Lap simulation (src/sim/lap.js) — GGV point mass

- Track = compass-walk segments → closed centreline → 700 stations with smoothed curvature.
- Corner limit: `V² (k − μρCLA/2m) = μg` — downforce-coupled; capped at terminal velocity.
- Forward pass: power `(P_ICE + ERS·η)/(m·V) − drag/m` with traction cap; backward pass: `μ_b(mg + DF) + drag`. Iterated to convergence (ΔV < 0.03 m/s).
- **Dynamic ride heights (§19)**: heave under aero load (`heave_ref` mm/g ÷ heaveStiff), pitch under ∆g at each station — the aero map is evaluated at the *deformed* platform.
- **Tyres (§22)**: μ vs temperature window (opt 95 °C ± 35), cold penalty, wear-driven fade; heat from lateral/longitudinal load, cooling with speed.
- **Limiter map (§29)**: every station labelled CORNER / GRIP / POWER+ERS / BRAKE / TRACTION by which constraint bound.
- DRS: auto-enabled where curvature < 1/800 sustained ≥ 150 m (zones policy, §63).
- Sectors, energy (MJ), top speed, tyre end-state.

## Robustness (src/sim/robustness.js)

Monte Carlo over wing-angle tolerance, ride-height tolerance, yaw (N(0,1.4°)), mass, air density. Reports mean/best/worst/spread, P(stall), P(balance excursion), histogram, and the seed used (reproducible, §91).

## Development correlation (src/core/dev.js)

Predictions run the *same* solvers. "Tested" results add a seeded, facility-dependent noise term — an explicitly documented abstraction (§44). Approval requires reading the predicted-vs-tunnel-vs-lap correlation table.

## Known simplifications (full list in LIMITATIONS.md)

No viscous CFD, no full 3-DOF chassis, no track-surface roughness, tyre model is a window approximation, wake is parameterised rather than solved.
